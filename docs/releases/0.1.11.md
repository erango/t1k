- Notification sounds: pick Mercury, Bell, Tick, Pulse, Chord, the macOS sound or Silent in Settings › Notifications & access, with a preview. Tabs that need you get the brighter sound; done and reminders get the softer one.
- Settings › Notifications & access shows whether notifications and Full Disk Access are on, with buttons straight to the right System Settings page, Ask again, and Restart T1K.
- If notifications are off, a card at the bottom of the sidebar says so, with a reminder dot on View options.
- Files opened in the preview panel can be selected (drag, double-click a word, triple-click a line, ⌘A) and copied with ⌘C or the Copy button.
- Dragging a tab or group shows a closed-hand cursor.
- Switching themes, or between dark and light, now recolors split panes and the quick terminal too.
- The theme list keeps its scroll position when you pick a theme, and scrolls with the mouse wheel.
- ⌥⌫ deletes a word in Claude Code's prompt.
- Bringing tabs over finds tabs open in Ghostty and Terminal.
